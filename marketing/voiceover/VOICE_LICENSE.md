# Egyptian Arabic narration provenance

- Voice/model: NAMAA Egyptian TTS, default synthetic Egyptian Arabic speaker (no person was cloned and no reference voice was supplied).
- Model: https://huggingface.co/NAMAA-Space/NAMAA-Egyptian-TTS — MIT license.
- Generated through the documented Gradio `generate_tts_audio` endpoint of https://huggingface.co/spaces/omarelshehy/NAMAA-Egyptian-Voice .
- Only the original promotional script was submitted; no private account data or user files were uploaded.
- Rendered seven segments, trimmed silence, adjusted speaking pace to 30 seconds, normalized narration to approximately −16 LUFS / −1.5 dBTP.
- No background music; no Quran recitation audio.
- `narration.wav` is the final master. Rejected test voices are not used.
- Synthetic speech is disclosed on the video end card. Automated transcription was used as a pronunciation sanity check, not a substitute for native-speaker review.
